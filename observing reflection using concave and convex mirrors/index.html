Understanding Curved Mirrors

Normal mirrors are completely flat. But what if a mirror is curved or bent like a giant bowl? These special mirrors change your reflection in super funny ways—making you look upside-down or very large!

🔍 What is actually happening here?

There are two main types of curved mirrors:

1. Concave Mirror (The Cave): This mirror curves inwards, just like entering a dark cave. When you look at it from close, your photo looks jumbo big! But if you stand far away, you look completely upside-down (inverted)!

2. Convex Mirror (The Bulge): This mirror bulges outwards like a fat tummy. It always shows a straight, tiny picture of you, but covers a huge area behind you.

Doctor's Secret: Dentists use concave mirrors to see your tiny teeth look super big! Cars use convex mirrors so drivers can see all the traffic behind them clearly.

The Shiny Spoon Test
📋 Material Required for this experiment:
🥄
1 Big Shiny Steel Spoon
💡
Good Room Light
👶
Your Own Smile!

🚀 What to do? (Steps):

Step 1: Take a clean, bright stainless steel spoon.
Step 2: Look at the inner side of the spoon (where you put your soup). This acts like a Concave Mirror. What do you see? Your face is upside-down! Move it closer—it turns straight and big!
Step 3: Now, flip the spoon and look at the back outer side. This acts like a Convex Mirror. Look closely! Your face stays straight up but looks very small and funny!

Quick Summary Points

Concave is Inwards: Works like a cave. Makes things look flipped upside-down when far, but huge when brought near.
