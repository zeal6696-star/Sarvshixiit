The Core Principle of Electromagnetism

An ordinary piece of iron does not attract metal parts on its own. However, passing an electric current through an insulated wire wrapped around an iron piece instantly converts it into a powerful temporary magnet known as an Electromagnet.

🔍 What is actually happening here?

When current flows through the copper wire coil, it generates a localized magnetic field. This field acts directly on the inner core, aligning the microscopic magnetic domains of the Soft Iron Nail. As long as the current stays on, the assembly acts as a strong magnet.

The lifting capacity of an electromagnet depends directly on two main factors:
1. The Current Strength: More electrical flow creates a stronger field.
2. The Number of Turns: Wrapping more loops increases the collective magnetic output.

Temporary Behavior: Soft iron is chosen because it loses its magnetic field instantly when the electric current is switched off. This on-demand magnetic switch is crucial for giant scrap crane systems and electronic sorting machines.

Step-by-Step Laboratory Guide
📋 Required Materials:
🔩
Large Soft Iron Nail (3 inches)
🔌
Insulated Copper Wire
🔋
DC Battery Pack & Key Switch
📎
Steel Paper Clips (for testing)

🚀 Experimental Execution Steps:

Step 1: Wind the insulated copper wire tightly around the soft iron nail in a single continuous direction, leaving free wire segments at both ends.
Step 2: Strip away the protective insulation coating from both free wire tips using sandpaper.
Step 3: Connect the exposed wire ends to the DC battery supply circuit via a switch key.
Step 4: Close the switch circuit to start the flow of current. Bring the tip of the iron nail close to the container of paper clips and measure the maximum count of clips lifted.

Quick Revision Insights
1
Temporary Nature: Electromagnets act as temporary magnets because their magnetism exists only while the current flows.
2
Soft Iron Selection: Soft iron is preferred over steel because it demagnetizes quickly without retaining residual magnetic effects.
3
Performance Factors: Lifting capacity scales linearly with increased wire turn density and higher electric current input.
